# AI Integrations

AI integrations empower you to connect **DecisionRules** with modern AI assistants like **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, or other **MCP-compatible** clients. Instead of toggling between applications, you can work directly within your preferred AI chat interface to interact with your rules and logic.

Through the **Authoring MCP Server**, your AI assistant can create, edit, explain, and test **business rules** within your **DecisionRules Space**, all using natural language. Whether you're constructing a new **Decision Table**, updating an existing **Decision Flow**, generating test data, or clarifying complex logic—your AI assistant becomes a seamless part of your decision modeling workflow.

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/2yxZBKNbZ-Q" %}

**DecisionRules** offers multiple **MCP servers**, each tailored to different needs:

* **Authoring MCP Server** – Provides secure access for your AI assistant to create, modify, test, and manage rules directly in your DecisionRules Space.
* **Distribution MCP Server** – Grants access to shared resources such as documentation, templates, and reusable functions, without requiring access to your full DecisionRules Space.

Choose the integration that aligns best with your workflow and accelerate your rule modeling and testing processes with the power of AI.
